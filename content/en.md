# About Me

I'm Ganjar, a frontend developer and tech lead with JavaScript as my main language. I spend most of my time building systems — not just UI, but the architecture behind it: database schemas, agent orchestration, and automation pipelines that keep running without me having to babysit them.

Over the past few years, my focus has shifted from just "building products" to "building systems that can teach themselves to work" — from a multi-agent framework for development automation (CAF), to a POS system with its own AI agent that helps shop owners manage stock and daily reports. I believe AI is most valuable not as a bolted-on feature, but when it actually removes the manual work that used to be exhausting.

These days I split my time between building products (Coderium, umkm-pos), designing custom AI agent systems, and sharing what I learn through content — for Indonesian developers who want to get better at using AI coding tools, and for business owners who want their own systems without having to become programmers.

## How I Work

- **Practical above everything** — a system has to actually get used, not just get demoed.
- **AI as a replacement for manual work**, not just a chatbot bolted on top.
- **Learn by building** — everything I teach, I've already applied on real projects.
